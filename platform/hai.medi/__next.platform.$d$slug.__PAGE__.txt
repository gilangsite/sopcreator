<p><strong>Dokumen Internal Resmi – Konfidensial</strong><br>
Departemen Creator Team, MEDTOOLS.ID<br>
Versi: 1.0<br>

<h2 id="ringkasan-eksekutif">RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF</h2>
<p>Dokumen ini adalah <strong>Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) resmi</strong> yang dirancang khusus untuk <strong>Creator yang memproduksi konten di platform @hai.medi (Brand MEDI WORLD)</strong>. SOP ini mengatur seluruh aspek: dari perencanaan konten, pembuatan, publikasi, hingga pengukuran performa sesuai dengan visi dan misi ekosistem MEDTOOLS.ID.</p>
<p>Platform @hai.medi adalah <strong>aset entertainment dan awareness driver utama</strong> dalam ekosistem MEDTOOLS. Sebagai brand Medi World dengan tagline <strong>"By Your Side, Every Step"</strong>, platform ini berfokus pada konten yang <strong>menghibur, relatable, dan viral</strong> untuk menjangkau audiens medis Indonesia seluas-luasnya dalam tahap <strong>AWARENESS</strong> dari marketing funnel AIDA. Konten di @hai.medi harus entertaining, funny, relatable dengan kehidupan mahasiswa/dokter, dan memiliki potensi viral tinggi tanpa mengorbankan positioning sebagai trusted medical platform.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="1-pendahuluan">1. PENDAHULUAN</h2>
<h3 id="1-1-fungsi-platform-hai-medi-dalam-ekosistem-medtools">1.1 Fungsi Platform @hai.medi dalam Ekosistem MEDTOOLS</h3>
<p><strong>@hai.medi</strong> adalah <strong>akun Instagram untuk brand Medi World</strong> yang berfungsi sebagai:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Awareness Driver &#x26; Traffic Booster (Pendorong Awareness Ekosistem)</strong>: Platform utama untuk menjangkau audiens medis Indonesia yang sangat luas melalui konten entertaining dan viral. Tujuan utama adalah <strong>maximize reach dan followers</strong> agar audiens aware dengan MEDTOOLS.ID ecosystem secara keseluruhan.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Entertainment Hub (Pusat Hiburan Medis)</strong>: Menyediakan konten yang menghibur, menggelitik, funny, relatable dengan daily challenge mahasiswa kedokteran dan dokter muda. Konten di sini adalah "entertaining, not lecturing" – provide value through entertainment.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Community Building &#x26; Brand Affinity (Membangun Komunitas &#x26; Affinitas Brand)</strong>: Membangun komunitas yang kuat, loyal, dan engaged dengan brand MEDTOOLS. Followers merasa "belong" ke komunitas medis Indonesia yang besar, supportive, dan fun.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Gateway to Deeper Ecosystem (Pintu Masuk ke Ecosystem yang Lebih Dalam)</strong>: Followers @hai.medi yang terkoneksi dengan konten entertaining akan naturally curious untuk explore brand lain di ecosystem (Academy, Store, Medimpact). @hai.medi adalah entry point.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Talent/Character Development (Pengembangan Talenta &#x26; Karakter)</strong>: @hai.medi adalah platform untuk develop dan feature talent/character komik "Medi" yang menjadi face dari MEDTOOLS ecosystem. Character ini menjadi relatable, beloved figure yang followers mengenal dan love.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="1-2-peran-creator-terhadap-platform-hai-medi">1.2 Peran Creator terhadap Platform @hai.medi</h3>
<p>Sebagai Creator yang bertugas di @hai.medi, tanggung jawab Anda meliputi:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Produksi konten entertaining &#x26; viral</strong> yang relatable dengan kehidupan mahasiswa FK dan dokter muda Indonesia, dengan humor yang segar dan authentic.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Ideasi konten yang kreatif, original, dan trend-aware</strong>, menunjukkan kemampuan untuk adapt tren TikTok/Instagram ke format yang on-brand dan engaging.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Eksekusi end-to-end</strong> dari perencanaan konten, production, editing, hingga publikasi dengan focus pada entertainment value dan shareability.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Character development untuk Medi</strong> (jika ada karakter/talent khusus) untuk menjadi relatable dan beloved oleh community.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Community engagement &#x26; interaction</strong> yang fun, approachable, dan build loyalty dengan followers.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Trend monitoring &#x26; adaptation</strong> untuk ensure konten always fresh, relevant, dan capitalize pada trending topic/format.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Support ecosystem cross-promotion</strong> dengan subtle mention ke brand lain (Academy, Store, Medimpact) ketika relevant, bukan forced.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="2-tujuan-platform-hai-medi">2. TUJUAN PLATFORM @HAI.MEDI</h2>
<h3 id="2-1-tujuan-dari-sisi-brand">2.1 Tujuan dari Sisi Brand</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Maximize brand awareness tentang MEDTOOLS.ID ecosystem</strong> melalui konten entertaining yang viral dan menjangkau audiens medis Indonesia sebesar-besarnya.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Establish Medi World sebagai personality yang fun, relatable, dan beloved</strong> di dalam komunitas medis Indonesia. Followers ingin follow bukan hanya karena konten edukatif, tapi karena emotional connection dengan brand.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Build ecosystem top-of-mind awareness</strong> – jika dokter/mahasiswa FK berpikir tentang "medical community content" di Indonesia, MEDTOOLS.ID / Medi World harus menjadi salah satu top-of-mind platform.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Differentiate dari kompetitor</strong> dengan positioning sebagai platform yang memahami medis student/doctor humor, culture, dan daily struggle. Relatable brand positioning.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Support brand storytelling</strong> untuk MEDTOOLS ecosystem secara keseluruhan melalui entertaining narrative dan character-driven content.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="2-2-tujuan-dari-sisi-edukasi-value">2.2 Tujuan dari Sisi Edukasi/Value</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Provide entertainment value &#x26; comic relief</strong> yang mahasiswa kedokteran dan dokter muda butuh dalam daily stressful journey mereka.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Build community feeling</strong> – followers feel like mereka part of bigger community dari dokter Indonesia yang memahami struggle mereka dan punya sense of humor about it.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Subtle value-add</strong> melalui entertainment – content tidak hanya funny tapi juga contain implicit learning/wisdom tentang medical life, culture, reality.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Normalize struggle &#x26; hardship</strong> dalam medical journey melalui humor – show bahwa banyak orang experience same thing, you're not alone (implicit support message).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Celebrate medical identity</strong> – content celebrate apa yang special/unique tentang being medis student/doctor di Indonesia.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="2-3-tujuan-dari-sisi-bisnis-konversi">2.3 Tujuan dari Sisi Bisnis &#x26; Konversi</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Maximize follower growth</strong> – primary KPI untuk @hai.medi adalah <strong>follower growth</strong> (secondary adalah engagement).</p>
</li>
<li>

<p><strong>Build audience asset</strong> – setiap follower adalah aset valuable untuk future campaigns, announcements, ecosystem promotions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Generate engagement &#x26; social proof</strong> – high engagement rate pada @hai.medi demonstrates bahwa MEDTOOLS community is alive, engaged, fun (social proof untuk ecosystem).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Support viral potential</strong> – konten @hai.medi yang viral akan generate free organic reach, traffic, dan awareness untuk whole ecosystem.</p>

<h2 id="3-target-audiens">3. TARGET AUDIENS</h2>
<h3 id="3-1-segmentasi-audiens-utama">3.1 Segmentasi Audiens Utama</h3>
<p><strong>Primary Audience #1: Mahasiswa Kedokteran Aktif (Maba – Senior)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Demografi</strong>: Usia 18-26 tahun, mahasiswa semester 1-12 di universitas Indonesia (FKUI, Unair, Undip, UNPAD, UNHAS, dll, termasuk universitas tier 2-3).</li>
<li><strong>Psychografi</strong>: Stressed tapi punya sense of humor, suka laugh at medical realities, value community &#x26; sense of belonging, very active di social media (Instagram, TikTok).</li>
<li><strong>Daily Life</strong>: Class, study, exam pressure, internship, jaga, financial stress, relationship challenge, diet struggle, sleep deprivation.</li>
<li><strong>Pain Point</strong>: Overwhelming workload, exam anxiety, imposter syndrome, financial pressure, missing fun college life, medical school struggle feel isolating.</li>
<li><strong>What They Love</strong>: Humor about their reality, relatable content, funny video, meme, trend, character-driven content, sense of humor about struggle.</li>
<li><strong>Content Preference</strong>: Short-form video (Reels, TikTok), fun, entertaining, trend-aware, character-driven. Prefer quality entertainment over educational content pada platform ini.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Primary Audience #2: Dokter Muda (Fresh Graduate – Junior Resident)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Demografi</strong>: Usia 25-32 tahun, fresh graduate atau dalam training program (intern, resident, fellowship).</li>
<li><strong>Psychografi</strong>: Still experience medical life struggle tapi di different level, appreciate humor about their current reality, want community connection dengan peers.</li>
<li><strong>Daily Life</strong>: Jadwal yang intense, patient care pressure, career decision, continuing education, still student mindset tapi dengan added responsibility.</li>
<li><strong>Pain Point</strong>: Burnout, work-life balance, financial pressure, career direction uncertainty, long hours, physical/mental exhaustion.</li>
<li><strong>What They Love</strong>: Humor about their reality (long shifts, bureaucracy, patient interaction), relatable content, connection dengan community yang understand struggle.</li>
<li><strong>Content Preference</strong>: Same preference seperti mahasiswa – entertaining, relatable, trend-aware, character-driven.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Secondary Audience: Medical Colleagues &#x26; Network (Other Healthcare Professional)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Family/spouse dari doctor yang want to understand medical life struggle.</li>
<li>Medical student atau fresh doctor di social circle yang influenced by peer sharing.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="3-2-level-pengetahuan-audiens">3.2 Level Pengetahuan Audiens</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Audiens sudah memiliki medical background atau dalam training</strong>, jadi mereka understand medical terminology dan concept.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Content tidak perlu explain basic medical terms</strong> – assume audiens understand (unless joke is about medical terminology itself).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Humor rooted dalam shared understanding</strong> dari medical education &#x26; practice – konten assume audiens know what medical school/doctor life is like.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Relatable humor over explanatory humor</strong> – konten succeed jika audiens go "OMG YES, EXACTLY THIS" not "hmm what does this mean?"</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="3-3-karakter-audiens-yang-relevan-dengan-konten">3.3 Karakter Audiens yang Relevan dengan Konten</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Mereka appreciate dark humor &#x26; gallows humor</strong> – humor about stress, hardship, struggle yang characteristic dari medical student/doctor culture.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Mereka value community &#x26; belonging</strong> – feeling like part of bigger group yang understand them is important. Want connection dengan other medical students/doctors.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Mereka are meme-literate &#x26; trend-aware</strong> – understand meme format, trending sound, viral format. Expect konten to be current &#x26; fresh.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Mereka are visual-first consumer</strong> – prefer video/meme/image over long text. Consume content fast, expect hook within 1-3 second.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Mereka are shareable-content lover</strong> – will share content dengan friends if find it funny/relatable. Shareability is key metric.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Mereka balance between being professional &#x26; having fun</strong> – appreciate humor but still want brand to respect medical profession. Not offensive humor, but intelligent, relatable humor.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="4-3-batasan-konten-do-s-don-ts-khusus-hai-medi-">4.3 Batasan Konten (Do's &#x26; Don'ts Khusus @hai.medi)</h3>
<p><strong>❌ KONTEN YANG WAJIB DIHINDARI:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Offensive humor</strong>: Tidak boleh humor yang demean, disrespect, atau offensive terhadap medical profession, gender, ethnicity, religion, atau group tertentu. Humor harus "smart" dan "intelligent" bukan mean-spirited.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Promoting dangerous behavior</strong>: Jangan normalize atau encourage dangerous practice (belajar sambil jaga dengan ekstrim, overmedication, unsafe procedure, etc.).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Inaccurate medical information</strong>: Even dalam humor context, jangan spread medical misinformation. Humor boleh, tapi fact harus akurat.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Disrespecting patients atau healthcare ethics</strong>: Don't joke about patient confidentiality breach, lack of empathy, atau unethical behavior dokter.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Low quality production</strong>: Blurry video, bad audio, sloppy editing diminish entertainment value dan brand credibility.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Excessive hard selling</strong>: Don't force promo ecosystem brand. Subtle mention OK, aggressive sell NO.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Copied/plagiarized content</strong>: Don't repost other creator content without credit. Be original.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Content yang dismissive terhadap mental health struggle</strong>: Don't joke about depression, burnout, suicide dalam medical student/doctor context dalam way yang trivialize struggle.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>External brand logo atau competitor mention</strong>: Keep konten focused on MEDTOOLS ecosystem, jangan promote unrelated brand.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>❌ <strong>Content yang alienate non-medical audience</strong>: While target adalah medis student/doctor, jangan make content SO inside-joke yang family/non-medical friend can't enjoy.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>✅ KONTEN YANG DIANJURKAN:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Intelligent, relatable humor</strong>: Humor yang rooted dalam shared understanding, not mean-spirited atau offensive. "OMG YES" moment.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Trend-aware &#x26; fresh</strong>: Content that is current, capitalize pada trending format, sound, topic. Not outdated reference.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Character-driven &#x26; personality-forward</strong>: Feature personality, character, relatable figure. Not generic health content.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Shareability-focused</strong>: Create content people WANT to share dengan friend. Viral potential high.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Authentic &#x26; genuine</strong>: Feel authentic &#x26; genuine, not overly polished atau fake. Medical student/doctor appreciate authenticity.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Community-focused</strong>: Build community feeling, celebrate shared culture, make followers feel belong.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>High production quality</strong>: Good lighting, clean audio, smooth editing. Entertainment quality matter.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Inclusive humor</strong>: Humor yang inclusive &#x26; celebrate diversity, not alienate group.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Hook strong di beginning</strong>: First 1-3 second must hook viewer. Entertainment content stand/fall on hook strength.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>✅ <strong>Encourage interaction</strong>: Content yang encourage comment, share, tag, participation drive engagement &#x26; community.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="5-sop-pembuatan-konten">5. SOP PEMBUATAN KONTEN</h2>
<h3 id="5-1-tahap-ideasi-konten">5.1 Tahap Ideasi Konten</h3>
<p><strong>Tujuan</strong>: Menghasilkan ide konten entertaining yang fresh, relevant, dan viral potential tinggi.</p>
<p><strong>Alur Kerja:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Trend Monitoring &#x26; Inspiration Gathering</strong> (Ongoing)</p>
<ul>
<li>Team constantly monitor:
<ul>
<li>TikTok trending sound &#x26; format (lihat apa yang viral di medical/student category)</li>
<li>Instagram Reels trending format</li>
<li>Meme template yang sedang viral</li>
<li>Twitter/X thread tentang medical student/doctor life</li>
<li>YouTube short yang viral</li>
<li>Competitor content (@doktertik, medical TikTok creator lain, medical meme page)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Document interesting idea di shared doc/Trello board untuk reference.</li>
<li>Set aside time daily untuk "scroll &#x26; inspiration" (mandatory 15-30 min per day untuk stay current).</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Weekly Ideation Session</strong> (Every Minggu, ideally Senin atau Selasa)</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Peserta</strong>: Social Media Manager + Editor + Illustrator + Talent (jika ada).</li>
<li><strong>Format</strong>: Brainstorming session, 60-90 menit.</li>
<li><strong>Process</strong>:
<ul>
<li>Each participant bring 3-5 idea untuk konten minggu ini.</li>
<li>Discuss idea bersama – expand, refine, combine idea.</li>
<li>Evaluate idea berdasarkan criteria:
<ul>
<li>Entertainment value (how funny/relatable is this?)</li>
<li>Trend relevance (is this current atau outdated?)</li>
<li>Shareability (will people share this?)</li>
<li>Production feasibility (can we produce this?)</li>
<li>Audience alignment (will our audience love this?)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Vote atau score idea, finalize top ideas untuk minggu ini.</li>
<li>Assign creator untuk each idea + due date.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Output</strong>: Concrete content ideas untuk minggu dengan assigned creator.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Trend Adaptation Strategy</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>For trending format/sound yang promising:
<ul>
<li>Identify medical angle atau how to adapt untuk medical context</li>
<li>Create 1-3 variation dari trending format</li>
<li>Execute quickly (trend window is short, usually 3-5 hari)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Keep reference library dari trending format yang work well untuk reference future.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Content Calendar Assembly</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Social Media Manager compile all approved ideas ke dalam weekly content calendar dengan:
<ul>
<li>Idea description, Format, Assigned creator, Due date, Posting date/time.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Share dengan team untuk visibility &#x26; coordination.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="5-2-tahap-riset-validasi-materi">5.2 Tahap Riset &#x26; Validasi Materi</h3>
<p><strong>Tujuan</strong>: Ensure konten adalah accurate, appropriate, on-brand, dan production-feasible.</p>
<p><strong>Alur Kerja:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Concept Validation</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Untuk setiap idea, ask:
<ul>
<li>Is this funny/entertaining? (if not, reconsider)</li>
<li>Is this relatable untuk target audience? (test dengan quick pulse check dengan friend dari target audience)</li>
<li>Is this original atau too similar dengan existing viral video? (quick YouTube/TikTok search)</li>
<li>Will MEDTOOLS audience appreciate this? (align dengan brand tone &#x26; audience)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Accuracy Check</strong> (for ideas dengan educational component)</p>
<ul>
<li>If konten contain any medical fact/reference, verify accuracy.</li>
<li>Ask expert/doctor jika unsure (better safe than spread misinformation juga dalam humor context).</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Appropriateness &#x26; Sensitivity Check</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Review konten untuk ensure:
<ul>
<li>Not offensive ke any group</li>
<li>Respect medical profession &#x26; patient</li>
<li>No harmful behavior normalization</li>
<li>Align dengan brand value (inclusive, supportive, professional)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If doubt, escalate ke Dept. Head untuk sensitivity review.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>

<h3 id="5-3-tahap-script-concept-development">5.3 Tahap Script &#x26; Concept Development</h3>
<p><strong>Tujuan</strong>: Translate idea jadi concrete script atau visual concept yang siap di-produce.</p>
<p><strong>Alur Kerja:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Script Writing</strong> (untuk Video Content)</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Structure</strong>:</p>
<pre><code>[0:00-0:02] HOOK: Attention-grabbing moment yang make viewer stay
[0:02-0:10] Setup: Establish situation atau expectation
[Main Content] Payoff, twist, punchline, relatable moment
[Conclusion] Final punchline or emotional beat
[0:40-0:45] Outro: Subtle CTA atau signature (follow, tag, check bio)
</code></pre>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Key Guidelines</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Hook MUST be strong (first 1-3 detik sangat critical untuk entertainment content)</li>
<li>Build anticipation atau expectation then subvert dengan funny payoff</li>
<li>Use relatable scenario atau character yang audience recognize</li>
<li>Pacing fast (don't linger di one beat terlalu lama)</li>
<li>Ensure punchline/payoff is clear (audience should "get the joke")</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Tone</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Funny &#x26; entertaining</li>
<li>Relatable &#x26; authentic</li>
<li>Smart humor (not offensive atau mean)</li>
<li>Casual &#x26; conversational (not overly polished)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Storyboard untuk Video</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Create simple visual storyboard dengan:
<ul>
<li>Key scene/shot di setiap timing</li>
<li>Main action/expression di setiap frame</li>
<li>Audio element (if any – music, sound effect, dialogue)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Simple sketch OK, jangan perlu artistic. Just untuk visualize flow.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Meme atau Carousel Layout Planning</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>For meme/single image:
<ul>
<li>Visual layout (where text, where image, font size)</li>
<li>Punchline clarity</li>
<li>Color scheme on-brand</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>For carousel:
<ul>
<li>Number of slide (keep concise, max 8-10)</li>
<li>Punchline structure (build dari slide 1, payoff di akhir)</li>
<li>Visual consistency across slide</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Concept Approval Before Production</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Submit script atau visual concept ke Social Media Manager atau Dept. Head.</li>
<li>Approval focus on: Entertainment value, Humor effectiveness, Brand alignment, Production feasibility.</li>
<li>Timeline: Approve dalam max 1 hari kerja.</li>
<li>Feedback usually quick unless major revision needed.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="5-4-tahap-produksi-visual-video">5.4 Tahap Produksi Visual &#x26; Video</h3>
<p><strong>Tujuan</strong>: Execute approved concept jadi entertainment content berkualitas tinggi.</p>
<p><strong>Alur Kerja:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Pre-Production Checklist</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>✓ Script finalized &#x26; approved</li>
<li>✓ Storyboard clear</li>
<li>✓ Talent confirmed (jika ada actor/character)</li>
<li>✓ Location scouted atau studio setup ready</li>
<li>✓ Equipment (camera, phone, lighting if needed) tested &#x26; ready</li>
<li>✓ Costume, props prepared (jika needed)</li>
<li>✓ Trending sound/music downloaded &#x26; ready (jika using trending audio)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Video Production Best Practice untuk Entertainment Content</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hook First 3 Second</strong>: Ensure yang happen dalam 3 detik pertama is compelling &#x26; make viewer want to continue.</li>
<li><strong>Maintain Pacing</strong>: Entertainment content perlu have good rhythm/pacing. Not too slow, not too frenetic.</li>
<li><strong>Authentic Performance</strong>: For talent-based content, authentic &#x26; genuine performance lebih penting daripada perfect acting. Relatable > polished.</li>
<li><strong>Clean Audio</strong>: Even untuk entertaining content, audio quality matter. Dialogue harus clear &#x26; audible.</li>
<li><strong>Good Lighting</strong>: Even dengan phone, good lighting make big difference. Natural light OK, jangan terlalu dark atau shadow.</li>
<li><strong>Stable Footage</strong>: Use tripod, gimbal, atau steady hand. Shaky video diminish entertainment value.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Editing Tips untuk Entertainment Content</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fast Pacing</strong>: Keep cut tight, don't linger. Entertainment content benefit dari quick cut.</li>
<li><strong>Music/Sound Design</strong>: Trending sound is bonus, but even simple music/sound effect can enhance humor.</li>
<li><strong>Text Overlay</strong>: Use text untuk emphasize punchline, add context, or create visual joke.</li>
<li><strong>Transition</strong>: Smooth transition matter. Consider trending transition atau simple fade.</li>
<li><strong>Special Effect</strong>: Use effect sparsely &#x26; smartly. Not overdo effect.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Trend-Responsive Production</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>For trending format: <strong>produce quickly</strong> (ideally same day atau within 1-2 days)</li>
<li>Have template ready untuk fast iteration (if using trending format, pre-prepare base template)</li>
<li>Be ready untuk produce 2-3 variation dari same trending format dengan different angle/twist</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Karakteristik Brand Voice:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>

<li>✓ Conversational &#x26; casual tone (not corporate/stiff)</li>

<li>✓ Show real emotion &#x26; struggle (not fake cheerfulness)</li>
<li>✓ Acknowledge difficulty dengan humor (don't minimize struggle)</li>
<li>✓ Genuine &#x26; authentic (audience can tell jika content is fake)</li>
<li>Contoh: "That moment when exam is over dan you realize half the answers were guess 🤦"</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Inclusive &#x26; Welcoming</strong> (Inclusive &#x26; Ramah)</p>

<li>✓ Celebrate diversity dalam medical field</li>
<li>✓ Humor yang inclusive, not alienating group</li>
<li>✓ Supportive tone (not judgmental tentang struggle)</li>
<li>Contoh: "Shout out to all medical student (regardless year/university) – we're all surviving together!"</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Smart &#x26; Clever</strong> (Smart &#x26; Clever)</p>
<ul>
<li>✓ Humor demonstrate understanding &#x26; intelligence</li>
<li>✓ Pop culture reference, meme literacy, trend awareness</li>
<li>✓ Not dumbed-down atau obvious humor</li>
<li>✓ Audience appreciate cleverness &#x26; wit</li>
<li>Contoh: "Medical student sleep schedule: expecting the world, settling for 3-hour nap" (understated humor)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Supportive &#x26; Empowering</strong> (Supportive &#x26; Empowering)</p>
<ul>
<li>✓ Underlying message is empowerment &#x26; validation</li>
<li>✓ Celebrate strength &#x26; resilience dari medical community</li>
<li>✓ Humor acknowledge struggle tapi also celebrate capability</li>

</li>

<h2 id="9-do-s-don-ts-creator-hai-medi">9. DO'S &#x26; DON'TS CREATOR @HAI.MEDI</h2>
<h3 id="9-1-wajib-dilakukan-do-s-">9.1 WAJIB DILAKUKAN (Do's)</h3>
<p>✅ <strong>Entertain First, Educate Second</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Content harus entertaining FIRST. If educational value ada, itu bonus tapi tidak mandatory.</li>
<li>Audience datang untuk laugh &#x26; relatable moment, bukan untuk learn (unlike Academy).</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Stay Current &#x26; Trend-Aware</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Monitor trend daily, adapt quickly</li>
<li>Incorporate trending sound, meme format, trend topic</li>
<li>Content yang outdated = not relevant = low engagement</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Create Relatable Content</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Content harus rooted dalam shared understanding dari target audience</li>
<li>"This is SO ME" moment adalah goal</li>
<li>Authenticity > perfection</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Build Community &#x26; Engagement</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Respond to comment &#x26; DM thoughtfully</li>
<li>Feature follower content &#x26; give credit</li>
<li>Create content yang encourage participation (tag, comment, share)</li>
<li>Show appreciation untuk community</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Maintain High Entertainment Quality</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Good hook dalam first 3 second</li>
<li>Strong punchline atau payoff</li>
<li>Good pacing &#x26; editing</li>
<li>Production quality matter untuk entertainment</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Be Original &#x26; Authentic</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Create original content, not copy other creator</li>
<li>Bring authentic personality &#x26; perspective</li>
<li>Add unique angle atau twist pada trending format</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Celebrate Medical Community</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Content celebrate what special tentang medical student/doctor</li>
<li>Supportive tone terhadap community</li>
<li>Inclusive humor (not alienate group)</li>
</ul>
<p>✅ <strong>Protect Medical Ethics &#x26; Professionalism</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Even dalam humor, respect medical profession &#x26; patient</li>
<li>Don't normalize dangerous atau unethical behavior</li>
<li>Don't breach patient confidentiality even dalam joke</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="9-2-dilarang-dilakukan-don-ts-">9.2 DILARANG DILAKUKAN (Don'ts)</h3>
<p>❌ <strong>Offensive atau Mean-Spirited Humor</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't demean, disrespect, atau offensive terhadap group (gender, ethnicity, religion, profession)</li>
<li>❌ Don't punch down (humor should punch up atau sideways, not down)</li>
<li>❌ Don't humor yang trivialize serious struggle (like mental health, burnout)</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Misinformation atau Dangerous Behavior</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't spread medical misinformation even dalam joke context</li>
<li>❌ Don't normalize dangerous practice (extreme pulling all-nighter, overmedication, unsafe procedure)</li>
<li>❌ Don't encourage unhealthy coping mechanism</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Plagiarism atau Copied Content</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't repost other creator content without permission &#x26; credit</li>
<li>❌ Don't copy competitor page concept exactly</li>
<li>❌ Don't use copyrighted audio/image without permission</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Low Quality Production</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't publish blurry video, bad audio, sloppy editing</li>
<li>❌ Don't post content yang technically poor quality</li>
<li>❌ Poor quality diminish entertainment value</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Forced atau Aggressive Promotion</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't make entertainment content primarily about hard-selling product</li>
<li>❌ Don't force ecosystem mention jika tidak natural</li>
<li>❌ Don't mislead audience dengan clickbait hook then pure promotion</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Disrespectful Terhadap Medical Profession</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't joke about patient confidentiality breach</li>
<li>❌ Don't promote lack of empathy terhadap patient</li>
<li>❌ Don't normalize unethical doctor behavior</li>
<li>❌ Don't dismiss importance dari medical practice</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Excessive External Brand Mention</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't promote competitor brand atau unrelated brand</li>
<li>❌ Don't put external logo prominent dalam content</li>
<li>❌ Keep focus pada MEDTOOLS ecosystem</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Ignore Follower Feedback</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't dismiss comment atau feedback dari audience</li>
<li>❌ Don't create content dalam vacuum without considering audience preference</li>
<li>❌ Engage WITH community, not AT community</li>
</ul>
<p>❌ <strong>Inconsistent Posting atau Abandonment</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Don't let account go silent untuk extended period (momentum loss)</li>
<li>❌ Don't post inconsistently (engagement algorithm penalize)</li>
<li>❌ Don't abandon stories atau daily updates</li>
</ul>
