SOP PANDUAN CREATOR – PLATFORM @HAI.MEDI (MEDI WORLD)

Dokumen Internal Resmi – Konfidensial
Departemen Creator Team, MEDTOOLS.ID
Versi: 1.0
Tanggal Berlaku: Januari 2026

RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF

Dokumen ini adalah Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) resmi yang dirancang khusus untuk Creator yang memproduksi konten di platform @hai.medi (Brand MEDI WORLD). SOP ini mengatur seluruh aspek: dari perencanaan konten, pembuatan, publikasi, hingga pengukuran performa sesuai dengan visi dan misi ekosistem MEDTOOLS.ID.

Platform @hai.medi adalah aset entertainment dan awareness driver utama dalam ekosistem MEDTOOLS. Sebagai brand Medi World dengan tagline "By Your Side, Every Step", platform ini berfokus pada konten yang menghibur, relatable, dan viral untuk menjangkau audiens medis Indonesia seluas-luasnya dalam tahap AWARENESS dari marketing funnel AIDA. Konten di @hai.medi harus entertaining, funny, relatable dengan kehidupan mahasiswa/dokter, dan memiliki potensi viral tinggi tanpa mengorbankan positioning sebagai trusted medical platform.

1. PENDAHULUAN

1.1 Fungsi Platform @hai.medi dalam Ekosistem MEDTOOLS

@hai.medi adalah akun Instagram untuk brand Medi World yang berfungsi sebagai:

  • Awareness Driver & Traffic Booster (Pendorong Awareness Ekosistem): Platform utama untuk menjangkau audiens medis Indonesia yang sangat luas melalui konten entertaining dan viral. Tujuan utama adalah maximize reach dan followers agar audiens aware dengan MEDTOOLS.ID ecosystem secara keseluruhan.

  • Entertainment Hub (Pusat Hiburan Medis): Menyediakan konten yang menghibur, menggelitik, funny, relatable dengan daily challenge mahasiswa kedokteran dan dokter muda. Konten di sini adalah "entertaining, not lecturing" – provide value through entertainment.

  • Community Building & Brand Affinity (Membangun Komunitas & Affinitas Brand): Membangun komunitas yang kuat, loyal, dan engaged dengan brand MEDTOOLS. Followers merasa "belong" ke komunitas medis Indonesia yang besar, supportive, dan fun.

  • Gateway to Deeper Ecosystem (Pintu Masuk ke Ecosystem yang Lebih Dalam): Followers @hai.medi yang terkoneksi dengan konten entertaining akan naturally curious untuk explore brand lain di ecosystem (Academy, Store, Medimpact). @hai.medi adalah entry point.

  • Talent/Character Development (Pengembangan Talenta & Karakter): @hai.medi adalah platform untuk develop dan feature talent/character komik "Medi" yang menjadi face dari MEDTOOLS ecosystem. Character ini menjadi relatable, beloved figure yang followers mengenal dan love.

1.2 Peran Creator terhadap Platform @hai.medi

Sebagai Creator yang bertugas di @hai.medi, tanggung jawab Anda meliputi:

  • Produksi konten entertaining & viral yang relatable dengan kehidupan mahasiswa FK dan dokter muda Indonesia, dengan humor yang segar dan authentic.

  • Ideasi konten yang kreatif, original, dan trend-aware, menunjukkan kemampuan untuk adapt tren TikTok/Instagram ke format yang on-brand dan engaging.

  • Eksekusi end-to-end dari perencanaan konten, production, editing, hingga publikasi dengan focus pada entertainment value dan shareability.

  • Character development untuk Medi (jika ada karakter/talent khusus) untuk menjadi relatable dan beloved oleh community.

  • Community engagement & interaction yang fun, approachable, dan build loyalty dengan followers.

  • Trend monitoring & adaptation untuk ensure konten always fresh, relevant, dan capitalize pada trending topic/format.

  • Support ecosystem cross-promotion dengan subtle mention ke brand lain (Academy, Store, Medimpact) ketika relevant, bukan forced.

2. TUJUAN PLATFORM @HAI.MEDI

2.1 Tujuan dari Sisi Brand

  • Maximize brand awareness tentang MEDTOOLS.ID ecosystem melalui konten entertaining yang viral dan menjangkau audiens medis Indonesia sebesar-besarnya.

  • Establish Medi World sebagai personality yang fun, relatable, dan beloved di dalam komunitas medis Indonesia. Followers ingin follow bukan hanya karena konten edukatif, tapi karena emotional connection dengan brand.

  • Build ecosystem top-of-mind awareness – jika dokter/mahasiswa FK berpikir tentang "medical community content" di Indonesia, MEDTOOLS.ID / Medi World harus menjadi salah satu top-of-mind platform.

  • Differentiate dari kompetitor dengan positioning sebagai platform yang memahami medis student/doctor humor, culture, dan daily struggle. Relatable brand positioning.

  • Support brand storytelling untuk MEDTOOLS ecosystem secara keseluruhan melalui entertaining narrative dan character-driven content.

2.2 Tujuan dari Sisi Edukasi/Value

  • Provide entertainment value & comic relief yang mahasiswa kedokteran dan dokter muda butuh dalam daily stressful journey mereka.

  • Build community feeling – followers feel like mereka part of bigger community dari dokter Indonesia yang memahami struggle mereka dan punya sense of humor about it.

  • Subtle value-add melalui entertainment – content tidak hanya funny tapi juga contain implicit learning/wisdom tentang medical life, culture, reality.

  • Normalize struggle & hardship dalam medical journey melalui humor – show bahwa banyak orang experience same thing, you're not alone (implicit support message).

  • Celebrate medical identity – content celebrate apa yang special/unique tentang being medis student/doctor di Indonesia.

2.3 Tujuan dari Sisi Bisnis & Konversi

  • Maximize follower growth – primary KPI untuk @hai.medi adalah follower growth (secondary adalah engagement).

  • Drive awareness & consideration untuk brand lain di ecosystem (indirect conversion path). Followers yang engaged dengan @hai.medi akan naturally curious explore Academy, Store, Medimpact.

  • Build audience asset – setiap follower adalah aset valuable untuk future campaigns, announcements, ecosystem promotions.

  • Generate engagement & social proof – high engagement rate pada @hai.medi demonstrates bahwa MEDTOOLS community is alive, engaged, fun (social proof untuk ecosystem).

  • Support viral potential – konten @hai.medi yang viral akan generate free organic reach, traffic, dan awareness untuk whole ecosystem.

3. TARGET AUDIENS

3.1 Segmentasi Audiens Utama

Primary Audience #1: Mahasiswa Kedokteran Aktif (Maba – Senior)

  • Demografi: Usia 18-26 tahun, mahasiswa semester 1-12 di universitas Indonesia (FKUI, Unair, Undip, UNPAD, UNHAS, dll, termasuk universitas tier 2-3).
  • Psychografi: Stressed tapi punya sense of humor, suka laugh at medical realities, value community & sense of belonging, very active di social media (Instagram, TikTok).
  • Daily Life: Class, study, exam pressure, internship, jaga, financial stress, relationship challenge, diet struggle, sleep deprivation.
  • Pain Point: Overwhelming workload, exam anxiety, imposter syndrome, financial pressure, missing fun college life, medical school struggle feel isolating.
  • What They Love: Humor about their reality, relatable content, funny video, meme, trend, character-driven content, sense of humor about struggle.
  • Content Preference: Short-form video (Reels, TikTok), fun, entertaining, trend-aware, character-driven. Prefer quality entertainment over educational content pada platform ini.

Primary Audience #2: Dokter Muda (Fresh Graduate – Junior Resident)

  • Demografi: Usia 25-32 tahun, fresh graduate atau dalam training program (intern, resident, fellowship).
  • Psychografi: Still experience medical life struggle tapi di different level, appreciate humor about their current reality, want community connection dengan peers.
  • Daily Life: Jadwal yang intense, patient care pressure, career decision, continuing education, still student mindset tapi dengan added responsibility.
  • Pain Point: Burnout, work-life balance, financial pressure, career direction uncertainty, long hours, physical/mental exhaustion.
  • What They Love: Humor about their reality (long shifts, bureaucracy, patient interaction), relatable content, connection dengan community yang understand struggle.
  • Content Preference: Same preference seperti mahasiswa – entertaining, relatable, trend-aware, character-driven.

Secondary Audience: Medical Colleagues & Network (Other Healthcare Professional)

  • Family/spouse dari doctor yang want to understand medical life struggle.
  • Medical student atau fresh doctor di social circle yang influenced by peer sharing.

3.2 Level Pengetahuan Audiens

  • Audiens sudah memiliki medical background atau dalam training, jadi mereka understand medical terminology dan concept.

  • Content tidak perlu explain basic medical terms – assume audiens understand (unless joke is about medical terminology itself).

  • Humor rooted dalam shared understanding dari medical education & practice – konten assume audiens know what medical school/doctor life is like.

  • Relatable humor over explanatory humor – konten succeed jika audiens go "OMG YES, EXACTLY THIS" not "hmm what does this mean?"

3.3 Karakter Audiens yang Relevan dengan Konten

  • Mereka appreciate dark humor & gallows humor – humor about stress, hardship, struggle yang characteristic dari medical student/doctor culture.

  • Mereka value community & belonging – feeling like part of bigger group yang understand them is important. Want connection dengan other medical students/doctors.

  • Mereka are meme-literate & trend-aware – understand meme format, trending sound, viral format. Expect konten to be current & fresh.

  • Mereka are visual-first consumer – prefer video/meme/image over long text. Consume content fast, expect hook within 1-3 second.

  • Mereka are shareable-content lover – will share content dengan friends if find it funny/relatable. Shareability is key metric.

  • Mereka balance between being professional & having fun – appreciate humor but still want brand to respect medical profession. Not offensive humor, but intelligent, relatable humor.

4. JENIS & FORMAT KONTEN

4.1 Tema Konten yang Diperbolehkan & Dianjurkan

Tema 1: Medical School Realities & Struggles (Realitas & Struggle Kuliah Kedokteran)

Konten yang reflect actual experiences dari mahasiswa kedokteran – exam stress, study struggle, relationship, sleep deprivation, financial challenge, etc.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST (build community)
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Carousel
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik (Reels/TikTok)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Medical student sleep schedule realistic vs expectation" (relatable video)
    • "How med student react to exam result" (funny reaction video)
    • "Things only medical student understand" (relatable list/carousel)
    • "Medical student budget allocation" (funny breakdown)

Tema 2: Doctor Life & Work Reality (Realitas Hidup Dokter)

Konten tentang doctor life – long shifts, patient encounter, bureaucracy, emotional moment, etc. Relatable untuk dokter muda dan aspirational/realizable untuk mahasiswa.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Video, Carousel
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik (Reels/TikTok)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Things doctor do when shift finally end" (funny action video)
    • "Patient ask you something, you realize degree not enough" (relatable humor)
    • "Doctor vs what people think doctor do" (comparison/expectation vs reality)
    • "Doctor emergency room moment (real vs movie)" (funny comparison)

Tema 3: Trend Adaptation & Meme Format (Adaptasi Tren & Format Meme)

Memanfaatkan trending format, sound, meme template, dan adapt ke medical context. This is about being CURRENT dan FRESH dengan trend.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS (viral potential)
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Feed Post (meme)
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik (video), single post (meme)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Popular TikTok sound yang di-adapt dengan medical context" (trending audio)
    • "Trending meme template yang relevant ke medical context" (meme)
    • "Viral challenge yang di-adapt dengan medical twist" (challenge variation)
    • "Trending transition/effect yang showcase medical life" (trendy format)

Tema 4: Character-Driven Content (Konten Karakter Medi)

Konten yang feature Medi character – funny personality, relatable situation, character development. Character menjadi relatable & beloved figure.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST (character brand building)
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Video Series, Carousel
  • Durasi: 15-60 detik (short series)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Medi day in the life as medical student" (character journey)
    • "Medi react to medical situation/trend" (character reaction)
    • "Medi give funny advice about medical life" (character personality)
    • "Medi series: Medical student journey" (serial content)

Tema 5: Medical Culture & Community Celebration (Perayaan Kultur Medis)

Konten yang celebrate culture, uniqueness, dan community dari medical profession. Feel-good content tentang medical community.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST (community building)
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Carousel, Video
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Only medical student can relate (with humor)" (community inside joke)
    • "Things we love about medical community" (celebration post)
    • "Medical student legacy & tradition" (cultural content)
    • "Salute to healthcare worker" (appreciation/celebration)

Tema 6: Funny Relationship & Social Content (Relasi & Konten Sosial Lucu)

Humor tentang relationship, dating, friendship, family sebagai seen through lens medical student/doctor life.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Carousel
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Dating a medical student – what to expect" (relatable humor)
    • "How medical student friend apologize for ghosting" (funny relatable)
    • "Family ask why you not home during exam" (relatable situation)

Tema 7: Behind-the-Scenes & Team Content (Behind-the-Scenes & Tim)

Konten yang show tim MEDTOOLS, process, fun moment di office, team dynamic. Humanize brand & build personal connection.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST (brand building)
  • Format: Reels, Stories, Carousel
  • Durasi: Short clips
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Day in the life ng MEDTOOLS team" (behind-the-scenes)
    • "Team react to medical trend" (team content)
    • "MEDTOOLS fun moment" (candid team content)

Tema 8: Interactive & Participation Content (Konten Interaktif & Partisipasi)

Konten yang encourage participation – poll, challenge, question, user tag, call for UGC, etc.

  • Tujuan Funnel: INTEREST, DESIRE (engagement & community)
  • Format: Stories (poll, quiz, question sticker), Reels with participation, Carousel with call-to-action
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Tag your medical school friend yang…" (tagging challenge)
    • "Poll: Medical student atau doctor preferred personality" (poll)
    • "What your medical student superpower" (question prompt)
    • "Send your funny medical school moment" (UGC call)

Tema 9: Educational-Entertainment Hybrid (Edukatif-Entertainment Hybrid)

Konten yang is entertaining FIRST tapi contain subtle educational value atau interesting fact. Not lecture, entertainment yang happen to teach.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST (value-add entertainment)
  • Format: Reels, TikTok, Carousel
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Weird medical fact yang will blow your mind" (interesting + entertaining)
    • "Medical skill you didn't know" (surprising + educational)
    • "Did you know about medical profession" (trivia format yang fun)

Tema 10: Subtle Ecosystem Mention & Cross-Promo (Cross-Promo Subtle)

Konten yang subtle mention Academy, Store, Medimpact, atau invite followers explore ecosystem. NOT hard sell, but natural mention.

  • Tujuan Funnel: ACTION (indirect conversion)
  • Format: Reels, Stories, Feed Post
  • Durasi: As needed
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "By the way, Academy punya course untuk…" (natural mention)
    • "Store product yang every medical student need" (soft mention)
    • "If you follow Medimpact, you know…" (casual reference)

4.3 Batasan Konten (Do's & Don'ts Khusus @hai.medi)

❌ KONTEN YANG WAJIB DIHINDARI:

  • ❌ Offensive humor: Tidak boleh humor yang demean, disrespect, atau offensive terhadap medical profession, gender, ethnicity, religion, atau group tertentu. Humor harus "smart" dan "intelligent" bukan mean-spirited.

  • ❌ Promoting dangerous behavior: Jangan normalize atau encourage dangerous practice (belajar sambil jaga dengan ekstrim, overmedication, unsafe procedure, etc.).

  • ❌ Inaccurate medical information: Even dalam humor context, jangan spread medical misinformation. Humor boleh, tapi fact harus akurat.

  • ❌ Disrespecting patients atau healthcare ethics: Don't joke about patient confidentiality breach, lack of empathy, atau unethical behavior dokter.

  • ❌ Low quality production: Blurry video, bad audio, sloppy editing diminish entertainment value dan brand credibility.

  • ❌ Excessive hard selling: Don't force promo ecosystem brand. Subtle mention OK, aggressive sell NO.

  • ❌ Copied/plagiarized content: Don't repost other creator content without credit. Be original.

  • ❌ Content yang dismissive terhadap mental health struggle: Don't joke about depression, burnout, suicide dalam medical student/doctor context dalam way yang trivialize struggle.

  • ❌ External brand logo atau competitor mention: Keep konten focused on MEDTOOLS ecosystem, jangan promote unrelated brand.

  • ❌ Content yang alienate non-medical audience: While target adalah medis student/doctor, jangan make content SO inside-joke yang family/non-medical friend can't enjoy.

✅ KONTEN YANG DIANJURKAN:

  • ✅ Intelligent, relatable humor: Humor yang rooted dalam shared understanding, not mean-spirited atau offensive. "OMG YES" moment.

  • ✅ Trend-aware & fresh: Content that is current, capitalize pada trending format, sound, topic. Not outdated reference.

  • ✅ Character-driven & personality-forward: Feature personality, character, relatable figure. Not generic health content.

  • ✅ Shareability-focused: Create content people WANT to share dengan friend. Viral potential high.

  • ✅ Authentic & genuine: Feel authentic & genuine, not overly polished atau fake. Medical student/doctor appreciate authenticity.

  • ✅ Community-focused: Build community feeling, celebrate shared culture, make followers feel belong.

  • ✅ High production quality: Good lighting, clean audio, smooth editing. Entertainment quality matter.

  • ✅ Inclusive humor: Humor yang inclusive & celebrate diversity, not alienate group.

  • ✅ Hook strong di beginning: First 1-3 second must hook viewer. Entertainment content stand/fall on hook strength.

  • ✅ Encourage interaction: Content yang encourage comment, share, tag, participation drive engagement & community.

5. SOP PEMBUATAN KONTEN

5.1 Tahap Ideasi Konten

Tujuan: Menghasilkan ide konten entertaining yang fresh, relevant, dan viral potential tinggi.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Trend Monitoring & Inspiration Gathering (Ongoing)

    • Team constantly monitor:
      • TikTok trending sound & format (lihat apa yang viral di medical/student category)
      • Instagram Reels trending format
      • Meme template yang sedang viral
      • Twitter/X thread tentang medical student/doctor life
      • YouTube short yang viral
      • Competitor content (@doktertik, medical TikTok creator lain, medical meme page)
    • Document interesting idea di shared doc/Trello board untuk reference.
    • Set aside time daily untuk "scroll & inspiration" (mandatory 15-30 min per day untuk stay current).

  2. Weekly Ideation Session (Every Minggu, ideally Senin atau Selasa)

    • Peserta: Social Media Manager + Editor + Illustrator + Talent (jika ada).
    • Format: Brainstorming session, 60-90 menit.
    • Process:
      • Each participant bring 3-5 idea untuk konten minggu ini.
      • Discuss idea bersama – expand, refine, combine idea.
      • Evaluate idea berdasarkan criteria:
        • Entertainment value (how funny/relatable is this?)
        • Trend relevance (is this current atau outdated?)
        • Shareability (will people share this?)
        • Production feasibility (can we produce this?)
        • Audience alignment (will our audience love this?)
      • Vote atau score idea, finalize top ideas untuk minggu ini.
      • Assign creator untuk each idea + due date.
    • Output: Concrete content ideas untuk minggu dengan assigned creator.

  3. Trend Adaptation Strategy

    • For trending format/sound yang promising:
      • Identify medical angle atau how to adapt untuk medical context
      • Create 1-3 variation dari trending format
      • Execute quickly (trend window is short, usually 3-5 hari)
    • Keep reference library dari trending format yang work well untuk reference future.

  4. Content Calendar Assembly

    • Social Media Manager compile all approved ideas ke dalam weekly content calendar dengan:
      • Idea description, Format, Assigned creator, Due date, Posting date/time.
    • Share dengan team untuk visibility & coordination.

5.2 Tahap Riset & Validasi Materi

Tujuan: Ensure konten adalah accurate, appropriate, on-brand, dan production-feasible.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Concept Validation

    • Untuk setiap idea, ask:
      • Is this funny/entertaining? (if not, reconsider)
      • Is this relatable untuk target audience? (test dengan quick pulse check dengan friend dari target audience)
      • Is this original atau too similar dengan existing viral video? (quick YouTube/TikTok search)
      • Will MEDTOOLS audience appreciate this? (align dengan brand tone & audience)

  2. Accuracy Check (for ideas dengan educational component)

    • If konten contain any medical fact/reference, verify accuracy.
    • Ask expert/doctor jika unsure (better safe than spread misinformation juga dalam humor context).

  3. Appropriateness & Sensitivity Check

    • Review konten untuk ensure:
      • Not offensive ke any group
      • Respect medical profession & patient
      • No harmful behavior normalization
      • Align dengan brand value (inclusive, supportive, professional)
    • If doubt, escalate ke Dept. Head untuk sensitivity review.

  4. Production Feasibility Check

    • Can we produce this dengan available resource?
    • Do we have talent, equipment, location needed?
    • Timeline realistic? (trend window consideration)
    • Budget within allocation?

5.3 Tahap Script & Concept Development

Tujuan: Translate idea jadi concrete script atau visual concept yang siap di-produce.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Script Writing (untuk Video Content)

    • Structure:

      [0:00-0:02] HOOK: Attention-grabbing moment yang make viewer stay
[0:02-0:10] Setup: Establish situation atau expectation
[Main Content] Payoff, twist, punchline, relatable moment
[Conclusion] Final punchline or emotional beat
[0:40-0:45] Outro: Subtle CTA atau signature (follow, tag, check bio)

    • Key Guidelines:

      • Hook MUST be strong (first 1-3 detik sangat critical untuk entertainment content)
      • Build anticipation atau expectation then subvert dengan funny payoff
      • Use relatable scenario atau character yang audience recognize
      • Pacing fast (don't linger di one beat terlalu lama)
      • Ensure punchline/payoff is clear (audience should "get the joke")

    • Tone:

      • Funny & entertaining
      • Relatable & authentic
      • Smart humor (not offensive atau mean)
      • Casual & conversational (not overly polished)

  2. Storyboard untuk Video

    • Create simple visual storyboard dengan:
      • Key scene/shot di setiap timing
      • Main action/expression di setiap frame
      • Audio element (if any – music, sound effect, dialogue)
    • Simple sketch OK, jangan perlu artistic. Just untuk visualize flow.

  3. Meme atau Carousel Layout Planning

    • For meme/single image:
      • Visual layout (where text, where image, font size)
      • Punchline clarity
      • Color scheme on-brand

      • Number of slide (keep concise, max 8-10)

5.4 Tahap Produksi Visual & Video

Tujuan: Execute approved concept jadi entertainment content berkualitas tinggi.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Pre-Production Checklist

    • ✓ Script finalized & approved
    • ✓ Storyboard clear
    • ✓ Talent confirmed (jika ada actor/character)
    • ✓ Location scouted atau studio setup ready
    • ✓ Equipment (camera, phone, lighting if needed) tested & ready
    • ✓ Costume, props prepared (jika needed)
    • ✓ Trending sound/music downloaded & ready (jika using trending audio)

  2. Video Production Best Practice untuk Entertainment Content

    • Hook First 3 Second: Ensure yang happen dalam 3 detik pertama is compelling & make viewer want to continue.
    • Maintain Pacing: Entertainment content perlu have good rhythm/pacing. Not too slow, not too frenetic.
    • Authentic Performance: For talent-based content, authentic & genuine performance lebih penting daripada perfect acting. Relatable > polished.
    • Clean Audio: Even untuk entertaining content, audio quality matter. Dialogue harus clear & audible.
    • Good Lighting: Even dengan phone, good lighting make big difference. Natural light OK, jangan terlalu dark atau shadow.
    • Stable Footage: Use tripod, gimbal, atau steady hand. Shaky video diminish entertainment value.

  3. Editing Tips untuk Entertainment Content

    • Fast Pacing: Keep cut tight, don't linger. Entertainment content benefit dari quick cut.
    • Music/Sound Design: Trending sound is bonus, but even simple music/sound effect can enhance humor.
    • Text Overlay: Use text untuk emphasize punchline, add context, or create visual joke.
    • Transition: Smooth transition matter. Consider trending transition atau simple fade.
    • Special Effect: Use effect sparsely & smartly. Not overdo effect.

  4. Trend-Responsive Production

    • For trending format: produce quickly (ideally same day atau within 1-2 days)
    • Have template ready untuk fast iteration (if using trending format, pre-prepare base template)
    • Be ready untuk produce 2-3 variation dari same trending format dengan different angle/twist

  • ❌ Don't post content yang technically poor quality
  • ❌ Poor quality diminish entertainment value

  • ❌ Don't normalize unethical doctor behavior
  • ❌ Don't dismiss importance dari medical practice

  • ❌ Don't promote competitor brand atau unrelated brand
  • ❌ Don't put external logo prominent dalam content
  • ❌ Keep focus pada MEDTOOLS ecosystem

❌ Ignore Follower Feedback

  • ❌ Don't dismiss comment atau feedback dari audience
  • ❌ Don't create content dalam vacuum without considering audience preference
  • ❌ Engage WITH community, not AT community

❌ Inconsistent Posting atau Abandonment

  • ❌ Don't let account go silent untuk extended period (momentum loss)
  • ❌ Don't post inconsistently (engagement algorithm penalize)
