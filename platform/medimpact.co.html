RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF

Dokumen ini adalah Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) resmi yang dirancang khusus untuk Creator yang memproduksi konten di platform @medimpact.co. SOP ini mengatur seluruh aspek: dari perencanaan konten, pembuatan, publikasi, hingga pengukuran performa sesuai dengan visi dan misi ekosistem MEDTOOLS.ID.

Platform @medimpact.co adalah pilar keempat dalam ekosistem MEDTOOLS dengan tagline "Connect, Collaborate, Grow!". Berdasarkan informasi dari dokumen sumber, @medimpact.co difokuskan pada konten yang mendorong AWARENESS dalam marketing funnel AIDA, dengan emphasis pada networking, kolaborasi, dan community building di antara profesional medis (dokter, mahasiswa, healthcare worker) di Indonesia. Platform ini berfungsi sebagai connector dan enabler untuk community medis Indonesia untuk saling terhubung, berbagi knowledge, berkolaborasi, dan tumbuh bersama.

1. PENDAHULUAN

1.1 Fungsi Platform @medimpact.co dalam Ekosistem MEDTOOLS

@medimpact.co adalah pilar keempat dari ekosistem MEDTOOLS yang berfungsi sebagai:

  • Community & Networking Platform (Platform Komunitas & Networking): Platform utama untuk memfasilitasi koneksi, kolaborasi, dan networking di antara profesional medis di Indonesia. @medimpact.co adalah space untuk medical professional meet, connect, collaborate, dan grow together.

  • Awareness & Reach Driver (Pendorong Awareness & Reach): Sebagai pilar AWARENESS dalam marketing funnel, @medimpact.co berfokus pada menjangkau audiens medis yang luas melalui konten yang compelling, shareable, dan relevant dengan medical community di Indonesia.

  • Collaboration & Knowledge Sharing Hub (Hub Kolaborasi & Knowledge Sharing): Platform untuk facilitate collaboration antara medical professional, encourage knowledge sharing, dan create opportunities untuk mutual growth dan support dalam medical community.

  • Brand Positioning & Differentiation (Positioning Brand Unik): Memposisikan MEDTOOLS bukan hanya sebagai educational atau commercial platform, tapi sebagai advocate for medical community yang truly understand their need dan provide platform untuk connect & grow.

  • Gateway untuk Deeper Engagement: Followers @medimpact.co yang tertarik dengan community-building message akan naturally interested dengan deeper offering di ecosystem lain (Academy untuk learning, Store untuk tools, etc).

1.2 Peran Creator terhadap Platform @medimpact.co

Sebagai Creator yang bertugas di @medimpact.co, tanggung jawab Anda meliputi:

  • Produksi konten networking & collaboration yang mendorong medical professional untuk connect, share, dan grow bersama dalam community MEDTOOLS.

  • Facilitate community stories & sharing – highlighting real story dari medical student/doctor tentang collaboration, mentorship, mutual support, professional growth.

  • Create compelling narrative tentang importance dari networking, collaboration, dan community dalam medical profession journey.

  • Ideasi konten yang showcase value dari community, connection, dan professional development dalam medical field.

  • Eksekusi end-to-end dari perencanaan konten, riset, produksi, hingga publikasi dengan focus pada community value & engagement.

  • Community engagement & relationship building – respond to community, facilitate discussion, celebrate member success, build belonging.

  • Support cross-platform ecosystem synergy dengan subtle mention & reference ke other MEDTOOLS brand ketika relevant.

2. TUJUAN PLATFORM @MEDIMPACT.CO

2.1 Tujuan dari Sisi Brand

  • Position MEDTOOLS sebagai advocate dan enabler untuk medical community – bukan hanya sebagai educational atau commercial entity, tapi as genuine supporter dari medical professional growth, collaboration, dan community.

  • Build brand affinity melalui community value – followers feel bahwa MEDTOOLS truly understand medical profession, value community, dan invest dalam community building (bukan hanya profit-driven).

  • Establish @medimpact.co sebagai go-to platform untuk medical professional networking & collaboration di Indonesia – place where medical student/doctor go to connect, share, grow, find opportunities.

  • Create ecosystem ecosystem moat – strong community loyalty & network effect make it harder untuk kompetitor. Network effects scale exponentially.

  • Support business goal dari MEDTOOLS melalui community-driven growth & organic reach dari word-of-mouth dalam medical community.

2.2 Tujuan dari Sisi Edukasi/Value

  • Provide value melalui community & networking – facilitate connection, knowledge sharing, mentorship, mutual support, opportunity discovery dalam medical community.

  • Celebrate professional growth & development – showcase story dari medical professional yang grow melalui collaboration, mentorship, community support.

  • Promote culture dari mutual support & knowledge sharing – encourage medical student/doctor untuk learn dari each other, share experience, mentor junior, support peer.

  • Normalize importance dari professional network & collaboration dalam medical career – network is not transactional, tapi essential untuk growth, learning, support.

  • Create safe space untuk medical community – place where medical professional dapat authentic share, ask for advice, find support, celebrate achievement, learn from peer.

2.3 Tujuan dari Sisi Bisnis & Konversi

  • Build community asset & network effect – large, engaged medical professional community adalah valuable asset untuk MEDTOOLS long-term.

  • Drive awareness & consideration untuk brand MEDTOOLS ecosystem melalui positive brand positioning & word-of-mouth dari engaged community.

  • Increase follower growth dari medical professional segment – secondary metric after engagement.

  • Facilitate organic cross-promotion dalam ecosystem – satisfied member di @medimpact.co naturally interested explore Academy, Store, Medimpact offering.

  • Support recruitment & partnership opportunity – strong medical community make it easier untuk recruit talent, find partner, grow business.

3. TARGET AUDIENS

3.1 Segmentasi Audiens Utama

Primary Audience #1: Medical Professional (Doctor & Medical Student)

  • Demografi: Usia 18-40 tahun, mahasiswa kedokteran/dokter di berbagai stage of career (student, fresh grad, resident, specialist, established practitioner).
  • Psychografi: Career-minded, value professional development & networking, seek mentorship/collaboration opportunity, want sense of community & belonging dalam profession.
  • Professional Interest:
    • Career development & advancement
    • Skill development & continuous learning
    • Networking & collaboration opportunity
    • Mentorship (both seeking & providing)
    • Professional challenges & support
    • Industry insight & trend
    • Opportunity discovery (job, research, project)
  • Pain Point:
    • Isolation atau loneliness dalam medical career
    • Limited access to mentorship atau guidance
    • Difficulty finding collaboration opportunity
    • Lack of professional network
    • Career direction uncertainty
    • Limited exposure to different specialization atau career path

Secondary Audience: Healthcare Professional lainnya

  • Nurse, technician, health staff yang value professional development & community.

Indirect Audience: Family & Support Network

  • Family member dari medical professional yang interested learn tentang medical profession & support.

3.2 Level Pengetahuan Audiens

  • Audiens adalah professional atau dalam professional training – understand medical terminology, professional context, industry challenges.

  • Diverse knowledge level – dari junior mahasiswa to senior specialist, from clinical to non-clinical healthcare professional.

  • Konten tidak perlu explain basic medical concept – assume professional medical background or in training.

  • Content harus be accessible ke diverse stage of career – relatable untuk both junior & senior, clinical & non-clinical.

3.3 Karakter Audiens yang Relevan dengan Konten

  • Mereka value authentic story & real experience – appreciate genuine narrative tentang professional journey, struggle, growth (not corporate-speak).

  • Mereka appreciate mentorship & guidance – seeking wisdom dari more experienced professional, also want opportunity to mentor junior.

  • Mereka value community & belonging – seek connection dengan peer yang understand professional challenge, celebrate mutual success, support each other.

  • Mereka are knowledge sharers – willing to share knowledge, experience, insight dengan community (both formal teaching & casual advice).

  • Mereka are opportunity seekers – looking untuk collaboration, research project, job opportunity, learning opportunity, mentorship.

  • Mereka are professional & aspirational – value career growth, professional development, continuous learning.

  • Mereka appreciate meaningful conversation – prefer substantive discussion over surface-level content.

4. JENIS & FORMAT KONTEN

4.1 Tema Konten yang Diperbolehkan & Dianjurkan

Tema 1: Professional Success Story & Career Journey (Kisah Sukses & Perjalanan Karir)

Konten yang showcase real story dari medical professional tentang career journey, achievement, lesson learned, growth path.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Long-form video (YouTube), Carousel, Reels, Stories
  • Durasi: 1-5 menit (YouTube), 5-10 slide (Carousel), 15-45 detik (Reels)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "From medical student to specialist – journey & lesson learned"
    • "How I found my niche dalam medical field"
    • "Overcoming burnout: one doctor's story"
    • "Building research career in resource-limited setting"

Tema 2: Mentorship & Guidance (Mentorship & Panduan)

Konten tentang mentorship – wisdom dari senior doctor, guidance untuk junior, mentorship opportunity, how to find mentor.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Video, Carousel, Live Q&A
  • Durasi: 1-3 menit (video), 5-8 slide (carousel)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Advice for junior doctor dari senior specialist"
    • "How to find good mentor in medical field"
    • "Mentorship opportunity: join our program"
    • "What I wish I knew as young doctor"

Tema 3: Collaboration & Teamwork (Kolaborasi & Kerja Tim)

Konten tentang importance dari collaboration, teamwork, interdisciplinary work dalam medical practice.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Video, Carousel, Reels, Stories
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik (Reels), 1-2 menit (video), 5-8 slide (carousel)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Power of interdisciplinary team dalam medical practice"
    • "How collaboration improve patient outcome"
    • "Join our collaborative research project"
    • "Teamwork make dream work: success story"

Tema 4: Knowledge Sharing & Learning Community (Berbagi Pengetahuan & Komunitas Belajar)

Konten yang facilitate knowledge sharing, peer learning, community discussion tentang medical topic, best practice.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Live session, Video, Carousel, Q&A Stories
  • Durasi: 30-60 menit (live), 1-3 menit (video)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Live case discussion dengan expert doctor"
    • "Best practice sharing dari successful medical program"
    • "Community learning: discuss challenging case"
    • "Research insight sharing session"

Tema 5: Professional Development & Skill Building (Pengembangan Profesional & Skill)

Konten tentang skill development, continuous learning, professional growth strategy, training opportunity.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST, ACTION
  • Format: Video, Carousel, Reels, Live workshop
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik (teaser), 1-5 menit (full video)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Essential skill every doctor should develop"
    • "Leadership training untuk medical professional"
    • "Research skill development workshop"
    • "Communication skill untuk better patient outcome"

Tema 6: Community Challenge & Initiative (Inisiatif & Challenge Komunitas)

Konten tentang community project, challenge, initiative yang encourage participation & contribution dari medical professional.

  • Tujuan Funnel: INTEREST, DESIRE, ACTION
  • Format: Reels, Video, Stories, Call-to-action post
  • Durasi: 15-45 detik (teaser), 1-3 menit (full info)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "30-day medical challenge: commit to growth"
    • "Research challenge: submit your study"
    • "Community service initiative: join us"
    • "Skill-sharing challenge: teach your skill"

Tema 7: Professional Network & Opportunity (Networking & Peluang Profesional)

Konten tentang networking opportunity, job opening, research position, collaboration opportunity, event untuk medical professional.

  • Tujuan Funnel: INTEREST, ACTION
  • Format: Carousel, Reels, Stories, Dedicated post
  • Durasi: Single post atau series
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Open research position: join our team"
    • "Collaboration opportunity: seeking partner"
    • "Medical conference: register now"
    • "Networking event untuk medical professional"

Tema 8: Celebration & Recognition (Perayaan & Pengakuan)

Konten yang celebrate member achievement, recognize contribution, highlight success story dalam community.

  • Tujuan Funnel: INTEREST (community building)
  • Format: Carousel, Reels, Stories, Dedicated post
  • Durasi: Variable
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Member spotlight: highlight successful doctor"
    • "Celebrate achievement: recognize community member contribution"
    • "Success story dari community member"
    • "Graduation celebration untuk medical student"

Tema 9: Community Value & Impact (Nilai & Impact Komunitas)

Konten tentang value dari community, impact yang dicreate oleh collaborative effort, why professional networking matter.

  • Tujuan Funnel: AWARENESS, INTEREST
  • Format: Video, Carousel, Reels, Stories
  • Durasi: 15-60 detik (short), 1-3 menit (long)
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Why professional network crucial untuk career"
    • "Impact dari collaborative research dalam medical community"
    • "How community support improve doctor wellbeing"
    • "Community power: together we're stronger"

Tema 10: Ecosystem Integration & Cross-Promotion (Integrasi Ecosystem & Cross-Promo)

Konten yang subtle mention & reference Academy, Store, atau Medtools offering ketika relevant untuk community member.

  • Tujuan Funnel: ACTION
  • Format: Stories, Carousel, Dedicated post
  • Contoh Topik:
    • "Academy course untuk professional development"
    • "Store product rekomendasi dari community member"

4.3 Batasan Konten (Do's & Don'ts Khusus @medimpact.co)

❌ KONTEN YANG WAJIB DIHINDARI:

  • ❌ Inaccurate atau unverified medical information: Even dalam sharing context, accuracy harus maintained.

  • ❌ Overly promotional atau sales-focused content: @medimpact.co adalah community platform, not sales channel. Subtle mention OK, aggressive sell NO.

  • ❌ Exclusive atau discriminatory content: Community should feel inclusive & welcoming untuk semua level, specialty, background medis.

  • ❌ Corporate speak atau inauthentic narrative: Medical professional appreciate authenticity. Avoid sounding too corporate atau marketing-like.

  • ❌ Competitive or divisive messaging: Don't create content yang pit doctor terhadap nurse, specialist vs general practitioner, etc. Community adalah unified.

  • ❌ Disrespectful terhadap member atau profession: Even dalam humor, maintain respect untuk medical profession & community member.

  • ❌ Low quality production: Professional audience expect professional production quality.

  • ❌ Content yang undermine patient care atau ethics: Don't normalize unethical behavior atau patient negligence (even dalam joke).

  • ❌ Copied atau plagiarized story: Always credit source, get permission, respect intellectual property dari member.

  • ❌ Content without clear community value: Every content should have clear benefit untuk community atau member.

✅ KONTEN YANG DIANJURKAN:

  • ✅ Authentic & genuine story: Real narrative dari real person tentang real experience.

  • ✅ Community-focused & member-centric: Content created FOR community member, not AT community.

  • ✅ Value-driven & helpful: Content provide value, insight, opportunity, atau support untuk member.

  • ✅ Inclusive & celebratory: Celebrate diversity, highlight various path & specialty, include various perspective.

  • ✅ Facilitate connection & collaboration: Content yang spark discussion, encourage connection, facilitate collaboration.

  • ✅ Professional & respectful tone: Maintain professional standard while being approachable & warm.

  • ✅ High production quality: Good audio, visual, editing – professional standard.

  • ✅ Member-spotlight & recognition: Feature member story, celebrate achievement, give platform untuk member voice.

  • ✅ Interactive & engaging: Encourage comment, discussion, participation dalam content.

  • ✅ Timely & relevant: Address current issue, opportunity, trend dalam medical community.

5. SOP PEMBUATAN KONTEN

5.1 Tahap Ideasi Konten

Tujuan: Menghasilkan ide konten networking & collaboration yang valuable untuk medical professional community.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Community Insight Gathering (Ongoing)

    • Regularly engage dengan community untuk understand:
      • What question they ask
      • What struggle they face
      • What opportunity they seek
      • What inspiration mereka need
    • Listen di comment, DM, discussion
    • Monitor medical industry trend & opportunity
    • Identify member success story worth highlighting

  2. Monthly Content Planning (Early bulan)

    • Peserta: Dept. Head + Social Media Manager + potentially community representative.
    • Agenda:
      • Review community pulse & member feedback dari bulan sebelumnya
      • Identify trending topic atau opportunity dalam medical community
      • Plan member spotlight atau success story untuk bulan
      • Identify collaboration atau partnership opportunity
      • Set content pillar untuk bulan (e.g., 40% success story, 30% knowledge sharing, 20% opportunity, 10% celebration)
    • Output: Monthly content strategy & key story to feature

  3. Weekly Ideation Session (Setiap minggu)

    • Peserta: Social Media Manager + Editor + community member (rotating)
    • Format: Brainstorming, 60-90 menit
    • Process:
      • Discuss community insight & feedback dari minggu sebelumnya
      • Brainstorm konten idea untuk minggu ini
      • Evaluate idea berdasarkan criteria:
        • Community value (is this valuable untuk member?)
        • Relevance (is this timely & relevant?)
        • Opportunity for connection (will this spark discussion & collaboration?)
        • Feasibility (can we produce this?)
      • Finalize content idea dengan assigned creator
    • Output: Weekly content list dengan assigned creator

  4. Member Story Sourcing

    • Actively reach out to potential story subject (doctor, student, healthcare worker) dengan compelling journey
    • Pitch story opportunity di community DM atau direct message
    • Develop relationship dengan potential storyteller
    • Create submission process untuk member yang want to share story

5.2 Tahap Riset & Validasi Materi

Tujuan: Ensure konten adalah accurate, authentic, appropriate, dan valuable untuk community.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Fact & Information Validation

    • If konten contain medical reference atau data, verify accuracy
    • Cross-check dengan credible source
    • Ask expert jika unsure

  2. Story Authenticity Check

    • For member story, verify authenticity dengan storyteller
    • Confirm permission untuk publish & use story
    • Ensure story reflect member's genuine experience (not exaggerated)
    • Get consent untuk mention name, institution (or keep anonymous if prefer)

  3. Community Value Assessment

    • Ask: "Will community member find this valuable?"
    • "Will this spark meaningful discussion atau connection?"
    • "Does this align dengan community value & inclusive spirit?"

  4. Appropriateness & Sensitivity Review

    • Ensure content respectful terhadap profession & community
    • Not divisive atau exclusive
    • Culturally & professionally appropriate
    • If mention specific institution/person, get permission

5.3 Tahap Script & Concept Development

Tujuan: Transform idea jadi concrete narrative atau visual concept yang siap di-produce.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Story Structure Development (untuk narrative-heavy content)

    • Story Arc: 
      BEGINNING: Introduce character/situation, set context
MIDDLE: Journey, challenge, key moment, learning
END: Resolution, impact, lesson, call-to-action
    • Key Elements:
      • Authentic detail yang make story relatable
      • Emotional beat yang connect dengan audience
      • Clear message atau lesson
      • Opportunity untuk community to engage (question, discussion prompt)

  2. Script Writing (untuk video content)

    • Structure: 
      [Intro] Hook & context (first 10 second crucial)
[Main] Story atau idea, detail, example
[Takeaway] Key message, insight, learning
[CTA] How to engage, join, learn more
    • Tone: Professional but warm, authentic, conversational (not corporate)

  3. Carousel Content Planning (untuk story/information carousel)

    • Determine number of slide (usually 6-10 slide)
    • Plan narrative flow across slide
    • Visual concept untuk each slide
    • Ensure compelling hook di slide 1 & strong payoff di akhir

  4. Concept Approval Before Production

    • Submit script atau concept ke Social Media Manager atau Dept. Head
    • Approval focus on: Story authenticity, community value, professional appropriateness, production feasibility
    • Timeline: Approve dalam max 1-2 hari kerja

5.4 Tahap Produksi Visual & Video

Tujuan: Execute approved concept jadi professional-quality community content.

Alur Kerja:

  1. Pre-Production Checklist

    • ✓ Script/story approved
    • ✓ Subject confirmed (for interview/story) & consent obtained
    • ✓ Location/setting determined
    • ✓ Equipment tested & ready
    • ✓ Interview question prepared (if applicable)

  2. Video Production Best Practice untuk Community Content

    • Authentic Performance: Real, genuine expression > polished acting. Community value authenticity.
    • Good Audio: Clear dialogue, minimal background noise. Audio quality crucial untuk professional audience.
    • Professional Lighting: Clean, professional visual quality. Avoid dim atau unprofessional appearance.
    • Stable Footage: Use tripod atau gimbal untuk stable shot.
    • Maintain Focus: Keep story focus central. Minimize distraction atau tangent.

  3. Interview Tips (for member story/expert discussion)

    • Preparation: Brief subject tentang topic, flow, key point
    • Comfort Level: Make subject comfortable, create safe space untuk authentic answer
    • Follow-up Question: Ask follow-up untuk depth & detail
    • Get good B-roll: Capture additional footage untuk use dalam editing

Tujuan: Ensure konten adalah professional-quality & community-appropriate sebelum publish.

  3. Final Approval

8. TONE OF VOICE & BRANDING

8.1 Tone of Voice untuk @medimpact.co

@medimpact.co voice adalah professional yet warm, community-focused, empowering, dan authentic. Brand personality adalah like a thoughtful community leader who genuinely care about member wellbeing, growth, dan success.

Karakteristik Brand Voice:

  1. Professional yet Approachable (Profesional namun Ramah)

    • ✓ Maintain professional standard & credibility
    • ✓ But communicate dalam warm, friendly manner
    • ✓ Not corporate atau stiff, but genuine & personable
    • Contoh: "As medical professional, we understand the challenge. Here's how community can help."

  2. Community-Focused & Member-Centric (Fokus Komunitas)

    • ✓ Everything about member benefit & value
    • ✓ Celebrate member achievement & voice member perspective
    • ✓ Create inclusive & welcoming space untuk all
    • Contoh: "Your story matter. Share how you grow through community support."

  3. Empowering & Inspiring (Memberdayakan & Menginspirasi)

    • ✓ Inspire member untuk connect, collaborate, grow
    • ✓ Show what possible melalui community & collaboration
    • ✓ Celebrate resilience & mutual support
    • Contoh: "Together, we achieve more. See how our community member transform their career."

  4. Authentic & Genuine (Authentic & Genuine)

    • ✓ Share real story, real struggle, real success
    • ✓ Acknowledge challenge & difficulty (not all rosy)
    • ✓ Celebrate authentic human moment
    • Contoh: "Being doctor is tough. But together, we're stronger."

  5. Knowledge-Focused & Growth-Oriented (Fokus Knowledge & Growth)

    • ✓ Share wisdom, insight, learning dari community
    • ✓ Facilitate knowledge sharing & peer learning
    • ✓ Promote continuous professional development
    • Contoh: "What we learn dari each other make us better doctor."

8.2 Karakteristik Konten yang Harus Konsisten

Professional Quality:

  • High production value (video, audio, visual)
  • Professional editing & presentation
  • Grammatically correct & well-written

Community Spirit:

  • Inclusive & welcoming tone
  • Celebrate diversity of specialty & background
  • Feature various perspective & voice

Authenticity:

  • Real story & real voice (not corporate speak)
  • Genuine narrative & emotional honesty
  • Member-driven perspective

Engagement-Focused:

  • Every content encourage discussion atau participation
  • Ask question, invite perspective, create conversation
  • Respond actively di comment

8.3 Voice Guidelines untuk Specific Situations

Saat Balas Komentar/DM:

  • ✓ Response Time: Max 24 jam (ideal: <12 jam)
  • ✓ Tone: Professional, warm, genuinely interested
  • ✓ Depth: Thoughtful response, not generic
  • Contoh: "Great point! This resonate dengan many member. How did you navigate this?"

Saat Feature Member Story:

  • ✓ Show genuine appreciation
  • ✓ Celebrate member vulnerability & sharing
  • ✓ Invite community to engage dengan story
  • Contoh: "We're honored to share Dr. Adi's story. His journey from struggle to success inspire many."

Saat Announce Opportunity atau Challenge:

  • ✓ Clear & enthusiastic about opportunity
  • ✓ Make benefit obvious
  • ✓ Make participation easy & inviting
  • Contoh: "Join our mentorship program! Whether junior atau senior, there's role for you."

Saat Handle Negative Comment atau Disagreement:

  • ✓ Respond professionally & respectfully
  • ✓ Don't get defensive
  • ✓ Invite constructive dialogue
  • Contoh: "Thanks for perspective. We value diverse view. Let's discuss further di DM."

9. DO'S & DON'TS CREATOR @MEDIMPACT.CO

9.1 WAJIB DILAKUKAN (Do's)

✅ Celebrate & Uplift Community Member

  • Feature member story, achievement, contribution
  • Give platform untuk member voice
  • Recognize success & effort

✅ Facilitate Genuine Connection & Collaboration

  • Create content yang spark meaningful discussion
  • Introduce member to each other
  • Facilitate collaboration opportunity

✅ Maintain Authenticity & Genuine Value

  • Share real story, not corporate marketing
  • Focus on member benefit, not brand promotion
  • Provide substantive, meaningful content

✅ Listen to Community & Respond to Need

  • Actively listen untuk community need & feedback
  • Create content yang address actual member question/struggle
  • Stay responsive dalam comment & DM

✅ Maintain Professional Quality

  • High production value
  • Grammatically correct & well-written
  • Professional standard dalam all aspect

✅ Be Inclusive & Welcoming

  • Welcome all specialty, background, level of experience
  • Feature diverse perspective & voice
  • Create space where all feel belong

✅ Support Ecosystem Vision

  • While community-focused, subtle support untuk ecosystem goal
  • Natural mention dari Academy, Store, Medimpact benefit
  • Help member see how ecosystem support their growth

9.2 DILARANG DILAKUKAN (Don'ts)

❌ Aggressive Promotion atau Sales-Focused Content

  • ❌ Don't make content primarily about selling product/service
  • ❌ Don't overpromote ecosystem brand
  • ❌ Don't use community platform as sales channel

❌ Inaccurate atau Unverified Information

  • ❌ Don't spread medical misinformation
  • ❌ Don't make unverified claim tentang professional opportunity
  • ❌ Don't present opinion as fact

❌ Exclusive atau Discriminatory Content

  • ❌ Don't create hierarchy atau division dalam community (specialist vs general, etc)
  • ❌ Don't exclude group atau voice
  • ❌ Don't discriminate based on background, specialty, level

❌ Inauthentic atau Corporate-Sounding

  • ❌ Don't use corporate speak atau marketing language
  • ❌ Don't present fake story atau exaggerate narrative
  • ❌ Don't sound overly promotional

❌ Disrespectful Terhadap Profession atau Member

  • ❌ Don't demean atau diminish medical profession
  • ❌ Don't dismiss member struggle
  • ❌ Don't mock atau belittle any specialty atau background

❌ Low Quality Production

  • ❌ Don't publish blurry video, bad audio, poor editing
  • ❌ Don't post typo atau grammar error
  • ❌ Professional audience expect professional quality

❌ Plagiarism atau Copied Content

  • ❌ Don't use member story without permission
  • ❌ Don't copy content dari other creator
  • ❌ Always credit source & get permission

❌ Divisive atau Negative Messaging

  • ❌ Don't create content yang divide atau pit community member against each other
  • ❌ Don't focus on negative atau complaint (focus on solution & support)
  • ❌ Don't promote unhealthy behavior atau burnout mentality

❌ Ignore Community Feedback atau Engagement

  • ❌ Don't dismiss comment atau member perspective
  • ❌ Don't create content dalam vacuum without considering member input
  • ❌ Don't post-and-ghost – engage actively dengan community
